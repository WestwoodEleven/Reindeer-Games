          Privacy policy

          Effective November 7, 2026

          Reindeer Games is a private, volunteer-run holiday game for participating families in the Emerald Bay neighborhood of Laguna Beach. This page explains what the site collects, who can see it, and the choices you have.

          What we collect

          • Sign-in details: your mobile number, or your email address if you sign in by email or Google. Google sign-in also shares your name.
          • Your family and house: your family name, house number and the map pin for your house.
          • Game activity: steals you log or claim, notes on them, and the times they happened.
          • What you post: feed messages, reactions, captions, and the photos and videos you upload.
          • Safety tools: reports you send and people you choose to block.

          We don’t collect your location from your phone, and we don’t use ads, analytics or tracking tools.

          Who can see it

          • Approved families can see family names, house numbers and pins, the game record, Rudolph Rumblings, and photos and videos. The newest steal stays hidden from everyone except the family who has Rudolph until he is stolen again. The organizer can see it only by turning on a spoiler setting.
          • The organizer can also see each person’s phone number or email (to approve people and keep the game safe), join requests, and reports.
          • People who aren’t approved see only the sign-in screen. Anyone signed in can check whether a single house number is already playing and see that family’s name.
          • Photo and video links can be opened by anyone who has the exact link, so please don’t forward them outside the game.

          We never sell or share your information with anyone outside the game.

          Where it’s stored

          The site runs on Google Firebase (sign-in, database and photo storage), hosted in the United States. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own security and privacy terms. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

          How long we keep it

          Each December’s game is kept as a keepsake so families can look back at past years. The organizer can delete any family, post, photo or record at any time.

          Your choices

          • Delete your own posts, photos and videos at any time from the ⋯ menu or the photo view.
          • Block someone to hide their posts, photos and notes for you.
          • Report anything inappropriate to the organizer.
          • Delete your account from the account menu (tap your family name at the top). This removes your sign-in, your membership, your posts, and your photos and videos. Steals you logged stay in the game record, without your contact details, so the trail and leaderboard stay accurate.

          Kids

          Reindeer Games is for families. Accounts are meant for adults, or teens with a parent’s permission. Children under 13 shouldn’t create their own accounts.

          Changes and contact

          If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above. Questions or requests: contact the organizer.
